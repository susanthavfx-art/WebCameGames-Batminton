Make your move

Motion: Enable your camera, stand back with your upper body visible, then move sideways. Swing your hand as the shuttle reaches your racket. High clears arrive above your head: raise your hand and swing overhead. Low drives arrive beside your body. Swing left or right to aim; keep your raised hand visible to the camera.

Keyboard: A / D or arrow keys move. Space swings. Hold Q / E to aim left / right. Or move your mouse over the court and click to swing.

Camera: The eye-level view sits behind your player, slightly over the shoulder. Choose Court overview in Settings if you prefer a wider view.

Rally scoring. Win by two, capped at 15 (quick match) or 30 (standard). Missions track your current match, not bonus points.
